❔ Help
  • 1 ungli — draw. 2 ungli — pinch zoom + pan.
  • 2 ungli se quick tap — Undo.
  • ✋ Hand tool (ya desktop par Space + drag) — canvas move karo. Zoom % par tap = Fit.
  • 🧅 Onion — pichli frames laal, agli frames hari dikhti hain.
  • Hold — kisi frame ko zyada der tak rokne ke liye (+).
  • Desktop keys: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (undo/redo), B pen, P pencil, M marker, E eraser, G fill, I picker, V move, H hand, [ ] size, ← → frames, N new frame, Enter play.
